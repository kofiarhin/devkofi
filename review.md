# DevKofi Review Standard

## Must fix

- Public messaging still positions DevKofi as a mentorship platform.
- A route, redirect, enquiry, authentication, or admin regression blocks the approved journey.
- Private product detail, credentials, or fabricated evidence appears publicly.
- Keyboard, focus, contrast, reduced-motion, or mobile behavior prevents use.
- A project is presented as shipped without verified evidence.
- Tests or build fail because of an in-scope change.

## Should fix

- Editorial hierarchy, spacing, typography, or motion is inconsistent.
- Loading, empty, error, success, or retry states are generic or unclear.
- Content is duplicated across pages instead of coming from a focused registry.
- A visual element adds cost without strengthening comprehension or proof.

## Okay to ship

- The approved journey is complete and verified.
- Existing operational contracts remain intact.
- Copy is concrete, honest, and free of unsupported claims.
- Authentic assets are used, or deliberate labelled asset slots remain.
- Responsive, accessibility, performance, and scope evidence is recorded.
