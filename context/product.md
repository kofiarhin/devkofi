# Product Context

## Product and promise

DevKofi is Kofi's creative technology studio. It turns useful ideas into working digital experiences and shows the engineering, visual craft, and decisions behind them.

## Target users and buyers

- Founders, small businesses, and teams commissioning product or creative technology work.
- Collaborators, recruiters, developers, and creators evaluating Kofi's practice.

## Problem and jobs

The legacy mentorship positioning no longer matches Kofi's active work. Visitors need to understand the combined practice, inspect credible work, and begin a qualified enquiry.

## Primary journey

Home → Work → Services or About → Start a Project → persisted enquiry.

## MVP scope

See `PRD.md`. The redesign covers public positioning, work, capabilities, studio story, lab, journal state, enquiry presentation, legacy redirects, and visual alignment of supporting/private surfaces.

## Exclusions

Mentorship marketing, destructive legacy removal, CMS work, invented evidence, merge, and deployment.

## Success criteria

The studio proposition is immediately clear, project states are honest, the enquiry path works, current operational contracts remain compatible, and verification evidence is recorded.

## Risks and unresolved details

Final photography, customer evidence, outcome metrics, and long-term Journal storage remain unresolved.
