# Dark Original Theme

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Decision

Keep the approved editorial layout and typography, but use a dark-only public canvas and restore the original DevKofi lime as the single accent.

## Reason

Manual review showed that the warm ivory sections felt disconnected from the existing DevKofi identity. The original dark and lime system has stronger brand continuity.

## Implementation

- Off-black is the page canvas.
- Zinc surfaces create section depth without light backgrounds.
- Lime is reserved for actions, active navigation, status, and focus.
- Full lime section fills are avoided so the accent remains intentional.
- The editorial serif and asymmetric layout remain.
