# PRD — DevKofi AI Engineering Studio

**Status:** Approved direction, implementation pending  
**Product:** DevKofi Website  
**Direction:** Founder-led AI Engineering Studio  
**Primary objective:** Reposition DevKofi from a general developer/personal portfolio into a focused AI engineering service business.

## 1. Product Vision

DevKofi will become the public-facing home of my AI engineering work and services.

The website should position me as an engineer businesses can hire to **design and build reliable AI systems, agentic workflows and AI-native products**.

The positioning should deliberately avoid the generic “AI automation agency” category.

DevKofi should communicate:

> **I engineer AI systems that do real work.**

The site should demonstrate both:

- **What I build** — client services, products and engineering work.
- **How I build** — the engineering systems, workflows, architecture and practices behind that work.

The experience should feel personal and founder-led. Copy should use **“I”**, not an artificial agency “we”.

## 2. Core Positioning

### Category

**AI Engineering Studio**

### Primary positioning

> **I engineer AI systems that do real work.**

### Supporting proposition

I design and build AI agents, intelligent workflows and AI-native products around real software, business processes and data.

The emphasis is on taking AI beyond prototypes through proper software engineering, integration, verification and production-oriented safeguards.

### Positioning principles

DevKofi should communicate:

- AI engineering rather than generic AI automation.
- Production systems rather than demos.
- Engineering outcomes rather than specific tools.
- Full-stack capability rather than isolated LLM integrations.
- Reliability and verification rather than “AI magic”.
- Human-controlled agentic systems where appropriate.
- Technical depth without overwhelming non-technical buyers.

## 3. Target Audience

The initial audience consists primarily of:

### Founders

Founders who have identified an AI opportunity but need someone capable of turning it into working software.

### CTOs / Technical Leaders

Teams that need additional AI engineering expertise without immediately building an internal AI engineering team.

### Product Teams

Companies looking to introduce AI capabilities into an existing product.

### Digital / Software Businesses

Businesses with existing software, data and workflows where AI could perform meaningful operational work.

The website should not position DevKofi as an inexpensive general-purpose automation provider for every small business.

## 4. Core Problem

AI prototypes are increasingly easy to create.

Turning those prototypes into dependable systems is significantly harder.

Real AI applications require engineering around:

- context
- data
- APIs
- tool use
- orchestration
- permissions
- application state
- human approval
- evaluation
- testing
- observability
- fallbacks
- security
- user experience
- deployment

DevKofi exists to engineer that surrounding system.

## 5. Services

### 5.1 Flagship — AI Systems Engineering

This is the primary DevKofi service and should receive the strongest visual and messaging emphasis.

#### Purpose

Design and engineer complete AI systems around real business requirements.

Potential capabilities include:

- AI agents
- multi-step agentic systems
- LLM applications
- tool-using agents
- context engineering
- RAG
- persistent memory
- APIs
- databases
- human-in-the-loop systems
- approval workflows
- evaluation
- verification
- observability
- frontend interfaces
- external integrations

The service should be presented around **problems and outcomes**, rather than a long technology checklist.

### 5.2 AI Product Engineering

Design and build AI-native products or substantial AI functionality within existing software.

Examples include:

- AI SaaS products
- AI copilots
- intelligent application features
- internal AI applications
- AI developer tools
- agentic applications

This service leverages DevKofi’s existing full-stack engineering background.

### 5.3 Agentic Workflow Engineering

Engineer complex workflows where AI agents interact with software, tools, data and people.

The differentiation from ordinary automation is important.

These are workflows requiring reasoning, state, context, verification, permissions or human decisions rather than simple trigger/action sequences.

### 5.4 AI Integration

Introduce AI capabilities into existing applications and systems.

Potential work includes:

- LLM integration
- AI APIs
- existing product integration
- data integration
- tool integration
- internal software integration
- AI-assisted functionality

### 5.5 AI Context & Knowledge Systems

Design the context infrastructure required for useful AI applications.

Potential capabilities:

- RAG
- semantic retrieval
- vector search
- structured context
- knowledge assistants
- persistent agent memory
- context APIs
- retrieval pipelines

### 5.6 AI Engineering Partner

Ongoing engineering support after an initial system has been built.

Potential responsibilities include:

- improving existing AI systems
- introducing new capabilities
- evaluations
- reliability improvements
- integrations
- workflow expansion
- architecture evolution

This should eventually provide a path from project engagements into longer-term relationships.

- clear hierarchy

- strong project screenshots

## 22. Centralised Theme System

Colours must not be scattered throughout individual components.

Create a central design-token/theme system.

Conceptually:

```text
theme
├── colors
│   ├── background
│   ├── surface
│   ├── text
│   ├── muted
│   ├── brand
│   ├── accent
│   ├── border
│   └── states
├── typography
├── spacing
├── radius
├── shadows
├── breakpoints
└── motion
```

Components consume semantic tokens rather than arbitrary colour values.

For example:

```text
background-primary
background-secondary
text-primary
text-secondary
brand-primary
brand-accent
border-subtle
```

The actual implementation should respect the existing DevKofi stack/configuration rather than introducing another theming system unnecessarily.

## 23. Image Strategy

The redesign should be **image-led but not image-heavy for its own sake**.

Priority:

1. Actual product screenshots
2. Actual project thumbnails
3. DevKofi profile photography
4. Architecture diagrams
5. Workflow/system visualisations
6. Purpose-built graphics where needed

Avoid generic stock photographs of robots, glowing brains, circuit boards or humanoid AI.

Every major image should provide evidence, context or personality.

### Header

Compact mobile header.

- **Left:** hamburger/menu control

Additional actions should remain minimal.

### Navigation Drawer

Navigation opens as a drawer from the **left side**.

Contains:

- Home
- Services
- Work
- Engineering Systems
- Products
- About
- Book a Call

The same hamburger control opens/closes the navigation, with appropriate close affordance and accessible state.

Requirements include:

- smooth but restrained transition
- focus management
- keyboard accessibility
- backdrop
- body scroll lock while open
- clear active route
- sufficiently large touch targets

## 27. Project Data Architecture

The existing project JSON should remain the source for reusable project metadata where practical.

It may need additional fields to support the new information architecture.

Conceptually:

```text
id
title
slug
type
category
featured
thumbnail
shortDescription
engineeringSummary
technologies
liveUrl
repositoryUrl
caseStudyUrl
aiEngineering
product
engineeringSystem
status
displayOrder
```

This enables the same source to power different filtered experiences.

For example:

- **Homepage:** `featured = true`
- **Work:** selected AI-engineering case studies
- **Products:** `product = true`
- **Engineering Systems:** `engineeringSystem = true`

Do not duplicate the same project information across page-specific hardcoded arrays.

The website should avoid:

- walls of text
- generic AI buzzwords
- unsupported outcome claims
- fake agency language
- excessive service lists

- architectural visuals

The visual direction should not sacrifice performance.

Requirements:

- responsive image sizing
- lazy loading below-the-fold media
- appropriately compressed project screenshots
- avoid unnecessary animation libraries unless justified
- minimise layout shifts
- optimise profile imagery
- keep animation GPU-friendly
- respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

- decorative images ignored appropriately
- accessible mobile drawer

- reduced-motion support

## 34. Success Criteria

A new visitor should be able to understand within approximately the first screen/initial interaction:

- **Who:** Kofi / DevKofi
- **What:** AI engineering
- **Speciality:** AI systems, agentic workflows and AI-native products
- **Difference:** engineering reliable systems rather than simply connecting AI tools
- **Proof:** actual products, systems and engineering work
- **Next action:** book a call

The redesign succeeds when DevKofi no longer feels like a general developer portfolio with AI projects added to it.

It should feel like:

> **The portfolio, engineering practice and commercial front door of an AI systems engineer.**

## 35. Out of Scope for Initial Redesign

Do not expand the first release unnecessarily with:

- full CMS
- customer portal
- AI chatbot
- complex lead scoring
- large blog platform
- account system
- dozens of service pages
- complete rewrite of every historical project
- new visual identity replacing DevKofi
- fabricated case-study metrics
- invented testimonials

Existing assets and infrastructure should be reused where appropriate.

## 36. Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is ready when:

- DevKofi is consistently positioned as an AI Engineering Studio.
- First-person language is used throughout.
- AI Systems Engineering is clearly the flagship service.
- Navigation contains Home, Services, Work, Engineering Systems, Products, About and Book a Call.
- Desktop hero uses text + existing profile image.
- Desktop content supports the alternating two-column visual system.
- Mobile sections consistently place imagery before supporting text.
- Mobile navigation uses the agreed left drawer.
- Existing DevKofi brand colours are preserved.
- Design tokens are centralised.
- Existing project data is reused instead of unnecessarily duplicated.
- Work and Products are clearly differentiated.
- Engineering Systems explains how the work is built.
- Existing screenshots/thumbnails provide the majority of visual proof.
- AI-focused work receives priority over historical generic portfolio projects.
- The site remains fast, responsive and accessible.
- The primary conversion path ends in booking a call.
