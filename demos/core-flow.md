# Core Demo Flow

## Audience and promise

A founder or small team can understand Kofi's combined product and creative practice, inspect credible work, and start a qualified project enquiry.

## Preconditions

- Public client loads.
- Project APIs may succeed, load slowly, return empty, or fail; the page must remain useful in every state.
- Final photography may be absent and represented by deliberate asset slots.

## Steps

1. Open Home and identify the creative technology studio positioning.
2. Follow selected work into Work.
3. Confirm Hibachi is labelled in development and shipped work is distinguished.
4. Open Services or About to understand capabilities and working approach.
5. Open Lab to discover current products and public tools.
6. Open Journal and see an honest curated/coming-soon state if no verified articles exist.
7. Open Start a Project, complete the existing supported fields, submit, and observe loading, error, or success feedback.

## Expected outcome

The visitor understands fit and can submit an enquiry through the existing persisted workflow.

## Important failure states

- Project/template API unavailable.
- Missing final photography.
- Invalid or failed enquiry submission.
- Telegram notification failure after successful persistence.
- Reduced-motion preference.

## Human review

Confirm copy accuracy, authentic assets, mobile composition, interaction quality, and that no project status or outcome is overstated.
